# CISA — Certified Information Systems Auditor

Issuing body: [ISACA](https://www.isaca.org/). The leading certification for IS/IT audit, control, assurance, and security professionals — focused on auditing, governance, and controls rather than hands-on offensive/defensive technical work.

## Who it's for / requirements

- A minimum of **5 years of professional experience** in information systems auditing, control, assurance, or security.
- Up to 3 years of the experience requirement can be waived through qualifying education or other ISACA-recognized certifications (see ISACA's experience substitution table).
- Requires passing the exam, submitting a completed application (experience must be verified), and adherence to the ISACA Code of Professional Ethics.
- Continuing education (CPE) is required annually to maintain the certification.

## Exam format

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | 150 multiple-choice questions, 4 hours |
| Format | Computer-based testing (CBT), available in multiple languages |
| Scoring | Scaled 200–800 |
| Passing score | 450 / 800 |
| Domains tested | 5 (below) |

## The 5 domains and weights

| # | Domain | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Information Systems Auditing Process | 18% |
| 2 | Governance and Management of IT | 18% |
| 3 | Information Systems Acquisition, Development, and Implementation | 12% |
| 4 | Information Systems Operations and Business Resilience | 26% |
| 5 | Protection of Information Assets | 26% |

Each domain below has a full outline (subtopics, definitions, exam tips) and a set of original practice questions with explanations:

### 1. [Information Systems Auditing Process](domains/01-information-systems-auditing-process.md) (18%) — [practice questions](practice-questions/domain-01.md)
IS audit standards, guidelines, and codes of ethics; risk-based IS audit planning; audit project management (scoping, scheduling); types of audits and assurance engagements; audit evidence collection techniques; sampling methodologies; reporting and communication of audit results; quality assurance and improvement of the audit process.

### 2. [Governance and Management of IT](domains/02-governance-and-management-of-it.md) (18%) — [practice questions](practice-questions/domain-02.md)
IT governance frameworks and best practices (COBIT); organizational structure, roles, and responsibilities related to IT; IT strategy and its alignment with organizational strategy; IT policies, standards, and procedures; enterprise risk management; IT resource/portfolio/program management; business continuity planning at the governance level; monitoring and reporting of IT performance.

### 3. [Information Systems Acquisition, Development, and Implementation](domains/03-information-systems-acquisition-development-implementation.md) (12%) — [practice questions](practice-questions/domain-03.md)
Project governance and management practices; business case development and feasibility analysis; system development methodologies (Agile, Waterfall, DevOps) and their control implications; control identification and design during development; testing methodologies (unit, integration, UAT); configuration and release/change management; post-implementation review.

### 4. [Information Systems Operations and Business Resilience](domains/04-information-systems-operations-and-business-resilience.md) (26%) — [practice questions](practice-questions/domain-04.md)
IT operations management (service management frameworks, ITIL); infrastructure and database management; incident and problem management; change/configuration/release management in production; end-user computing controls; data governance in operations; business impact analysis; disaster recovery planning and testing; system resilience and availability considerations.

### 5. [Protection of Information Assets](domains/05-protection-of-information-assets.md) (26%) — [practice questions](practice-questions/domain-05.md)
Information security frameworks, standards, and programs; identity and access management controls; network and endpoint security controls; data classification and encryption/PKI; physical and environmental controls; security awareness training; security incident response and management; evidence collection and forensics in a security-incident context.
